Our kitchen

Prepared the old-fashioned Indian way — with affection.

Saffron is committed to serving you and your family the very best. We build each dish on the freshest produce the season offers, and cook it the way it has always been cooked at home: patiently, and with care.

Our meals are made with kosher salt and minimal oil or butter — the depth comes from freshly ground aromatic spices instead. Every plate carries bold flavor without ever being greasy.

Fresh Vegetables & Lentils

Seasonal produce and whole lentils, simmered slowly rather than shortcut.

Handmade Appetizers

Samosas folded, chaats layered and pakoras battered to order in our kitchen.

Organic, Never-Frozen Meats

Marinated the day they are cooked, then finished over open tandoor heat.
